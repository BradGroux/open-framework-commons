# Charter

- **Status:** Draft for owner review
- **Steward:** Brad Groux

## Purpose

Open Framework Commons keeps the Open Framework Ecosystem coherent without turning its independent products into one system.

It provides a shared philosophical foundation so people and agents can recognize what every ecosystem product stands for while still following the product-specific guidance appropriate to their work.

## Ecosystem

Commons serves:

- AI-Native Operating Framework;
- Influence Operating Framework;
- AI Dev Days; and
- Relationship Operating Framework, when it is created.

These products have equal provenance and importance. They may serve different audiences, use different structures, and evolve on different schedules.

## What Commons owns

Commons owns only:

- shared principles;
- shared boundaries;
- shared expectations for honest research and review;
- the relationship among ecosystem products; and
- guidance for referencing Commons without surrendering product independence.

## What Commons does not own

Commons does not own a product's purpose, audience, method, lifecycle, examples, research questions, Mission Control design, roadmap, release, or implementation.

Commons cannot silently change another product. Each product decides whether and when to adopt a Commons revision.

## Open commitment

Commons and the products it serves are intended to be open source under the MIT License. They should improve through use, research, contribution, and accountable revision rather than claims of permanent completeness.
