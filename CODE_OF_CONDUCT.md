# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in Open Framework Commons should be respectful, specific, and
useful. Welcome different backgrounds and experience levels. Critique ideas and
evidence without attacking people. Preserve privacy, consent, attribution, and
the independent authority of each ecosystem product.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate
misrepresentation, publishing private information without permission, or
disrupting discussion to prevent good-faith participation.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
public spaces used to represent this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For an immediate public-content problem or private conduct report, use
[GitHub's report-abuse process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
If a threat, private disclosure, or harmful instruction also affects repository
or publication security, follow the private route in the
[security policy](SECURITY.md). Do not publish sensitive details in an issue.

The founding steward may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when conduct is harmful or persistently
disruptive. Responses should be proportionate, explain the applicable standard
when safe, and protect the privacy of people who report concerns.
