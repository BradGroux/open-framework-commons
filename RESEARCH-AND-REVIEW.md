# Research and Review

## Purpose

The ecosystem should evolve through honest learning. Research and review help people understand what is established, what is being tested, and what remains uncertain.

## Evidence language

- **Experience:** an observation from practice with its context and limits.
- **Community feedback:** a signal from participants or adopters that may reveal needs, friction, or disagreement.
- **Formal research:** a cited external source considered for a specific claim and context.
- **Real-world application:** documented use that records what happened, including failure, restraint, or unexpected outcomes.

An example or document review can show whether guidance is understandable. It does not by itself prove that the guidance works in practice.

## Product ownership

Each product owns its research questions, sources, findings, examples, and changes. Commons owns only the shared expectation that evidence and uncertainty are described honestly.

When learning may affect multiple products, the originating product should identify the possible shared impact. Commons changes only after the steward decides the finding is genuinely shared.

## Review questions

- Can an intended reader or agent understand the guidance?
- Does the guidance remain people-first and tool-independent?
- Could it be misread as permission for harmful, deceptive, or unaccountable action?
- Are examples clearly examples rather than requirements or proof?
- Are research claims supported and limited appropriately?
- Does the proposed change belong here or in one product?

## Material change disposition

For a material shared change, record a compact argument in the existing issue or decision record:

- Is the statement a chosen value, an interpretation of guidance, or a claim about an effect? A chosen commitment needs an accountable rationale, not invented scientific proof.
- What supports it, in which context, and with what limitations? One product’s positive experience is not evidence that it works everywhere. Explain any inference beyond the observed context.
- Why does the proposed meaning apply to each of the five products, and what relevant differences, counterexamples or unknowns remain? Similar wording alone does not establish shared usefulness.
- What does the strongest counterexample change? Consider unwanted burden, privacy limits, competing principles, and responsible restraint as well as successful cases.
- Does the steward accept, revise, keep local, defer or reject the proposal, and why? Preserve dissent and identify what evidence or event would justify reconsideration.

Keep this proportionate. Factual and spelling fixes do not need a research dossier. A clarification need not wait for use in all five products. Do not impose numerical evidence quotas, a central research database or a universal experiment. Real application evidence remains product-owned; cite or summarize it only within its publication limits.

The [worked cases](project/reviews/content-process-cases-2026-09-05.md) exercise ambiguity and adverse outcomes. They test whether the text supports a reasoned decision; they are explicitly fictional and do not prove field effectiveness.

## Living work

Products should say what is established, what is in progress, and what should be researched next. Contradictions and failed assumptions are useful evidence and should remain visible until resolved.
