# Decision 0002: Calendar editions and content review

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-05
- **Authority:** Brad Groux, Commons steward
- **Edition:** 2026.09.05

## Decision

Use YYYY.MM.DD calendar editions, UTC publication dates and a numbered same-day correction suffix. Preserve all historical identities and require explicit release notes about compatibility. Resolve issues #27–#30 through clearer human boundaries, bounded conflict resolution, evidence dispositions and explicit adoption with exceptions. Repository tooling and publication controls resolve #16–#25 without becoming shared product requirements.

## Rationale and alternatives

A dated edition tells readers which body of guidance they reviewed. Retaining semantic numbering was possible but would not itself define what a content change means. Calendar numbering still requires the substantive compatibility assessment in Governance. Retagging earlier editions was rejected because it would obscure existing adoption identities.

The human-boundary wording makes the existing non-coercive intent explicit and aligns with downstream safeguards. It is substantive clarification: readers who previously interpreted openness as permission to disclose or contribution as an entry condition must reconsider those interpretations. It is not declared universally compatible merely because that intent was already preferred.

The new review process concerns Commons decisions. It does not impose a workflow, evidence quota, software, or automatic adoption on another product. Existing formal research, examples and historical reviews are unchanged. No field-effectiveness claim is made.

## Product applicability and counterevidence

| Product | Relevant decision and limit |
| --- | --- |
| AI-Native Operating Framework | Requests for support and bounded publication of work examples remain legitimate; local authority, professional boundaries and method stay local. |
| Influence Operating Framework | Clarification agrees with its explicit privacy/consent limits and contribution without entitlement; outreach methods are not imported into Commons. |
| AI Dev Days | Beginner questions need not be earned. Event and teaching evidence cannot establish universal effectiveness. Its older Commons pin remains valid. |
| Relationship Operating Framework | A contribution creates no debt; disengagement can be responsible. Relationship-specific consent and contact processes stay local. |
| Focus Operating Framework | Capacity and responsible stopping remain personal decisions; stewardship does not require preserving every commitment or sharing private journals. |

These are compatibility observations, not new adoption decisions. Current product evidence is linked in the [audit](../project/audits/2026-09-04-codebase-audit.md). Product-local methods differ, so a shared mandatory contribution sequence was rejected. Worked counterexamples and a deferred proposal are in the [scenario review](../project/reviews/content-process-cases-2026-09-05.md).

## Disposition and reconsideration

Accept these bounded clarifications and Commons-local review rules. No substantive dissent has been recorded in this decision; preserve any later dissent in its issue. Revisit if readers still infer earned access to help, involuntary reciprocity, permission to disclose, or cross-product authority, or if product use provides counterevidence about the guidance. Each product may independently adopt, defer or document a deviation from this exact edition.
